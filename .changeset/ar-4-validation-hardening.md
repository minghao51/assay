fix(ar-4): validation hardening batch (AR-V1, AR-V3, AR-V4). The RE2-subset checker was DuckDB-verified against the entire rejected pool (pinned CLI 1.5.5) and aligned with real behavior: `\v` (vertical tab), leading-`]` class members (`[]a]`, `[]]`, `[^]a]`), zero-width-token quantifiers (`^*`, `$+`, `\b{2}`, `\A?`, `\z{2}`, and `a(?i)*` via a transparent `(?flags)` directive), and zero-padded `\x{…}` in the Unicode range now compile; genuinely out-of-range hex, `(?i)*`, and everything DuckDB rejects stay refused with now-true reasons (AR-V1). `compileContract` is now total against throwing/revoking getters and proxies over contract **and** schema input (guarded reads, guarded `Array.isArray`/`Object.keys`, snapshotted arrays), returning typed binding errors instead of raw throws (AR-V3). The type classifier recognizes DuckDB's real `DESCRIBE` spellings `TIMESTAMP_S`/`TIMESTAMP_MS`/`TIMESTAMP_NS` (→ `temporal`) and names `INTERVAL`/`BIT` as recognized-but-unbindable rather than "unknown engine type spelling" (AR-V4, with a real-`DESCRIBE` agreement leg). No golden-SQL/canonical bytes change: the newly accepted patterns previously produced no plan at all, and the newly classified types appear in no golden contract.
