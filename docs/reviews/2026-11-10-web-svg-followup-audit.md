## 4. Risk-based revision to the plan

Do not combine temporal formatting, dirty replacement and edit gating into one “small P0” worker assignment. They have different semantics, file boundaries, decision prerequisites and rollback risks.

Recommended order **if the maintainer selects runtime work**: baseline/preflight (WF-00), then pipeline label honesty (WF-02) as the first runtime candidate while read-only WF-01P produces the temporal matrix. Scalar temporal implementation (WF-01) follows only after explicit matrix approval and refreshes its baseline, then small presentation/context slices (WF-03/04). Dirty replacement starts with its own design (WF-05) and may run as read-only planning beside the first writer; implementation (WF-06) requires a second sign-off. Layout research (WF-08) may also be read-only; alternate center layouts (WF-09) stay blocked. Row-range QoL (WF-07) is lower priority than correct values and safe replacement. WF-10 verifies the **selected subset**, not every future capability in the atlas.

Safety demand may justify moving WF-05 ahead of polish, but the recommendation is **not** a reversal of the maintainer's prior deferral. Options and exact approvals are in the map. A participant/AT study remains the way to learn whether larger IA changes are warranted, not a box agents can tick by taking screenshots.
