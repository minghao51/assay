# ADR 0010: Open-Core Local Product, Paid Collaboration and Governance

## Status

Proposed

## Context

Basic data viewing and SQL are increasingly commoditized. Stronger commercial value appears in collaboration, governance, controlled access, enterprise integrations, and managed workflows.

## Decision

Keep the local developer experience broadly free/open: web, VS Code, CLI, local inspection, SQL, diff, validation, and transforms. Monetize team workspaces/reviews/shared rules and enterprise SSO/SCIM/audit/connectors/gateway/policy/KMS/private deployment.

## Consequences

The free utility becomes the adoption engine. The commercial hypothesis should remain proposed until adoption and buyer behavior validate it.
