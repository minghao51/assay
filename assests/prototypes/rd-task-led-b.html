      What this artifact is — and is not

      • Synthetic data only. No file is opened, no query runs, no bytes are written, nothing is stored or transmitted.
      • Built from source at bba491a to test an information architecture, not to prove the product is intuitive. No participant testing was performed; no human usability evidence exists for this design.
      • It is not a claim that the redesign is implemented, approved, or that every SVG board capability ships. Deferred decisions (SQL sizing, overlay-aware profile, dirty-session guard, DCR inspector, Help consolidation) are out of scope.
      • Copy quoted from the current source is labelled; annotations are prototype commentary and do not describe shipped behaviour unless they cite a file.
      • Light scheme only at a 1440px frame (plus a 900px narrow mode). The product must still be checked in dark scheme, at 1024×700 / 1440×900 / 1920×1080, at 200% zoom, keyboard-only and with assistive technology.
